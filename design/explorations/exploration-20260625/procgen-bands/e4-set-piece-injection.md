# Set-Piece Injection (generation flavor)
**Category:** Generation flavors (applied on top of any archetype)

## The flavor
Hand-authored prefab rooms — a **vault**, a **boss arena**, a **trap gauntlet** — seeded into otherwise-procedural space. The bulk of the band is still stitched piece-by-piece by the generator; the set-piece is a single curated chunk dropped in among them, fully designed by hand so it lands *exactly* the way the author intended every time it appears. It is the cheapest, highest-leverage anti-sameness win in procgen because it spends a little hand-authoring to break the "I've seen this corridor before" fatigue with a guaranteed memorable beat — and it composes on top of *any* of the archetypes (a vault works in discrete rooms, in a cavern, in a maze). Very common in shipped games: *Spelunky*'s special rooms (shops, the Moai, altars) injected into its procedural level templates, *The Binding of Isaac*'s fixed special rooms (treasure, boss, secret, shop) dropped into a procedural floor graph, and *Diablo*-lineage "pre-set tiles" stamped into random dungeons.

## How it fits THE FAR YARD bands
Set-pieces give a procedural band **landmarks** — a player who finds the vault on this dive *remembers this dive*. Each is a curated **risk/reward spike** inside the extraction loop: a vault = a dense loot payout behind a hard fight or a hazard wall, which sharpens the core **"push deeper or extract now?"** decision (the vault is exactly the thing worth the longer `dist_to_gate` walk home, with the dive clock still ticking). It is the natural home for **curated opposition combos** from the opposition spread — a *trap gauntlet* is a hand-placed run of Fixtures (pop-up spikes → crusher piston → sweeping laser), a *boss arena* is a bounded room sized for The Hunter or a mini-boss with its own sightlines, a *mimic-loot vault* pairs the `6-mimic-loot` opposition with a fat visible reward (the bait). Knowledge/Lore set-pieces fit too — a quiet "archive" room with a readable terminal or a Cyrus lore beat, a non-combat payout on the Knowledge track per the GDD's currency split. The author controls the *whole* room, so the curated thing reads cleanly instead of emerging by accident.

## Generation approach (on the real bandgen system)
The cheap win is that **the generator is already piece/socket based**, so a set-piece is *just an authored zone piece* (per `B1_zone_piece_format`) — a `PackedScene` with a `Geometry` `TileMapLayer` and tagged `Marker2D` sockets — that happens to be hand-designed and content-rich. It enters the same `piece_catalog.gd` and flows through the same `BandGenerator` placement: `_weighted_pick_index` can already draw it, `_alignment_offset` mates it flush, `band.fits()` rejects overlaps, `socket_sealer.gd` walls its unused sockets. No new placement engine — set-pieces ride the existing machinery.

The only addition is an **injection policy** layered onto selection, so a set-piece isn't placed by raw weight like a corridor:
- Tag set-piece entries in `ZonePieceData` (e.g. `is_set_piece`, `min_depth_index`, `max_per_band`, `unique`).
- After `DepthGrader.grade()` assigns `depth_index`, run an injection pass that **depth-gates** which set-pieces are eligible (a high-tier vault only past `min_depth_index`, mirroring how `JunkPlacer` keys loot off `depth_norm`), **frequency-caps** them (`max_per_band`), and **dedupes** (`unique` → once per run). Eligible-and-uncapped set-pieces get a reserved slot in the grow loop or a targeted swap of a placed generic room at qualifying depth.
- **Determinism:** every choice — *which* set-piece, and *where* — stays on the `RNG` autoload at a stable draw site/order, on integer math, so `test_bandgen_determinism.gd`'s seed-reproducibility holds and a given (seed + config) yields the same vault in the same spot.

## Flavor knobs
- **Injection frequency / cap** — expected set-pieces per band, and `max_per_band` per type.
- **Depth gating** — `min_depth_index` / `depth_norm` band each set-piece is eligible in (vaults deep, a tutorial-ish gauntlet shallow).
- **Uniqueness** — `unique` (once-per-run) vs. repeatable.
- **Guaranteed vs. random** — a forced "every run has exactly one vault" slot vs. probabilistic appearance.
- **Reward tier** — the loot/Lore tier the set-piece is allowed to grant, coupled to its danger.

## Synergies & tensions
Composes with **every** archetype — it's a stamp, not a floorplan, so it layers onto discrete-rooms, caverns, mazes, hub-and-spoke alike. It is the cleanest host for curated opposition combos and for Knowledge/Lore beats. The main tension is the **hand-authoring cost**: each set-piece is bespoke content (geometry + opposition placement + reward), so the budget caps how many exist, and too few means a player sees the *same* vault every run — the sameness you were fixing reappears at the set-piece layer. Pairs well with a **wear/decay** flavor to cheaply multiply variety: one authored vault + decay states (flooded / collapsed / looted) reads as several without re-authoring the layout.

## Open questions
- **Authoring budget (Director — scope/effort):** how many distinct set-pieces ship for M1's content pass? A vault + a gauntlet + a boss arena is the minimum to prove the flavor; below that they feel rote within a few runs. Recommendation: author 3–4 to validate, treat the count as an ongoing content lane, not a one-time spend.
- **How many before they feel rote (Director — fun):** what's the appearance rate that keeps a set-piece special? Too frequent and the vault stops being a landmark. Needs playtest tuning of `max_per_band` + frequency — flag for the M-gate.
- **Socket compatibility guarantee (technical):** a hand-authored room must expose sockets the generator can actually mate. Do we constrain set-pieces to the canonical 2-cell socket width (B1's fixed width) and require ≥1 socket, or add a fallback that retries/relocates if a depth-gated set-piece can't attach at its target slot? Recommend: enforce ≥1 canonical-width socket as an authoring lint, plus a graceful "skip and log" if injection fails so a band never fails to generate over a set-piece.
- **Reserved-slot vs. swap (technical):** inject as a first-class grow-loop draw, or post-generate by swapping a placed generic room of the right footprint at qualifying depth? Swap is simpler but needs footprint/socket compatibility; reserved-slot integrates with growth but complicates the target-count loop. Lean swap for M1 (smaller blast radius), revisit if it constrains placement too much.
- **Guaranteed-vault pacing (Director — fun/vision):** should every run be *guaranteed* a flagship set-piece (reliable payoff, risks predictability) or kept probabilistic (more variance, some runs feel flat)? Recommendation needs a Director call against the desired run-to-run variance.
