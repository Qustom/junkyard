# Extraction-as-Objective
**Category:** Extraction mechanic depth

## The mechanic
Make a **rare deep exit the reward itself**, not merely the way home. Most bands hold the ordinary gate (step up, press interact, bank — today's loop). A *special* exit is a curated set-piece you have to *find* and *reach*, and using it grants something the ordinary gate never does: a **permanent shortcut** to a deeper band (Spelunky-Tunnel style), a one-time **loot cache** gated behind the exit, or a **meta-unlock** (a recipe, a Knowledge beat, a band reveal). The point: "where's the exit?" stops being trivial bookkeeping and becomes a goal. Seeking the special exit is the dive's objective; banking is the consolation. It directly serves the GDD's deep-dive fantasy and meta-progression unlocks — the deep exit *is* the shortcut/cache the GDD already promises.

## What exists today
Extraction today is dumb-by-design and singular: one `ExtractGate` per band at a hand-authored spot, instant single-press hand-off to `GameState.extract_and_end_run()` (`entities/gate/extract_gate.gd`, E1). It is purely a *means to leave + bank* — every gate is identical, every exit is "safe haul home." There is **no notion of a special exit, a reward-bearing exit, or a found exit**. Nothing reads depth at the exit; nothing branches the reward by *which* exit you used. The natural delivery vehicle already exists in spec form: **set-piece injection** (procgen e4) lets a hand-authored, depth-gated, frequency-capped, dedup'd prefab room ride the existing `BandGenerator`/`piece_catalog` machinery. A "special exit" is just a set-piece whose payload is *a second, better gate*. What's missing is (a) a second gate variant with reward semantics, and (b) the depth-gated injection policy from e4 actually wired.

## How to fit it in
1. **Special-exit set-piece.** Author a `deep_exit` set-piece (e4): tagged `is_set_piece`, `min_depth_index` high (deep only), `max_per_band = 1`, `unique`. Inject it after `DepthGrader.grade()` so it only lands past a depth threshold (`depth_grader.gd` already assigns `depth_index`/`depth_norm`). The room contains a `SpecialExitGate` extending the E1 contract.
2. **Reward variants** (one knob picks per band): **permanent shortcut** — using it records a meta flag so future runs can *start* at that deeper band (Spelunky shortcut; survives in meta-state, never run-state); **cache** — a fat loot drop the exit unlocks on use; **meta-unlock** — a recipe/Knowledge grant. The gate still banks the haul (reuses `extract_and_end_run`), then layers its bonus on top.
3. **Map-intel tie-in (s4).** Because bands are seeded-deterministic, intel's "high-value-room tip" can sell *the location of this run's special exit* — converting e5 into a paid objective. Knowledge could reveal special exits standing-free (GDD: Knowledge = safe routes / master key).
4. **Quota/deep-dive tie-in.** The shortcut compresses future quota grind (start deeper = richer loot sooner); the cache is a quota burst. Both reward *going for it* over a safe early bank.
5. **RunConfig knob + telemetry.** `special_exit_enabled` (default off = today's single-gate baseline = the permanent control), plus `special_exit_reward_mode`. Telemetry: `special_exit_spawned {band, depth}`, `special_exit_found`, `special_exit_used {reward_mode}`, and the causal question — do players push deeper / extract later when a special exit exists vs. blind baseline? If deepest-depth-reached doesn't move, it's decorative.

## Research (cited)
- **Spelunky 2 shortcuts** — Mama Tunnel builds **permanent** level-skip shortcuts in exchange for money/items over several runs; notably the *only* non-cosmetic persistent unlock, a deliberate "reward without trivializing the run" middle ground. The canonical permanent-shortcut model and the explicit trade-off (skip early levels but miss the gear you'd have gathered) ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/spelunky-2-how-to-unlock-all-shortcuts-mama-tunnel-terra-guide/), [Spelunky Wiki](https://spelunky.fandom.com/wiki/Shortcuts)).
- **Escape from Tarkov — conditional extracts** — extraction *is* the point of the raid, and special exits carry conditions/cost (pay Roubles for the car extract, flip a switch, find a key, backpack-size limits) and aren't available every raid. Direct prior art for "a better/special exit you must *qualify* for" in the extraction-shooter genre ([eft-ammo wiki](https://www.eft-ammo.com/wiki/tarkov-extraction-points), [BoostRoom](https://boostroom.com/blog/escape-from-tarkov-extraction-guide-how-to-find-and-use-extracts)).
- **Dead Cells — timed doors & rune exits** — each biome has a standard exit *and* an optional rune-gated/secret exit to high-value areas; timed doors reward fast clears with gold/cells/blueprints. The "two exits, the special one is the reward" structure, with the reward gated behind skill/condition rather than just RNG ([Dead Cells Wiki — exits](https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Biomes), [timed doors](https://deadcells.fandom.com/wiki/Time,_Killstreak_%26_No-Hit_Doors)).

Synthesis: e5 sits on a **reward-of-exit** axis — permanent (Spelunky), one-run conditional (Tarkov car/Dead Cells timed door), or unlock-bearing. Pairing it with map-intel (e4/s4) lets the economy *price* knowing where the good exit is.

## Open questions
- **Permanent shortcut vs. one-run reward (Director — vision/economy).** A permanent deeper-start shortcut is the strongest fantasy but compresses the early game forever after (Spelunky accepts this with a gear trade-off); a one-run cache is safer but lower-stakes. Recommend shipping the **cache** variant first (cheapest, no meta-boundary churn) and prototyping the permanent shortcut behind the knob — needs a Director call on whether THE FAR YARD wants persistent skip-deeper progression.
- **Rarity tuning (Director — fun, M-gate).** How rare keeps it special? Too common and the "special" exit is just the normal exit. Tune `max_per_band`/injection frequency + `min_depth_index` at playtest; over-frequency kills the landmark feeling (same risk e4 flags).
- **Overlap with set-piece + intel systems (technical/scope).** e5 is *defined* in terms of e4 set-pieces and s4 intel — it has little independent surface. Is e5 a separate feature or just "a reward kind for e4 + an intel product for s4"? Recommend treating it as a **composition** (a deep-exit set-piece template + a gate variant + two telemetry fields), not a new system — keeps the blast radius tiny. Director to confirm it earns a distinct line item vs. folding into e4/s4.
- **Reaching vs. using.** Permanent shortcuts in Spelunky cost *resources over runs*; do we want the deep exit to demand a payment/condition (Tarkov-style) to *use*, or is reaching it deep enough the cost? Flag — interacts with quota pressure.
