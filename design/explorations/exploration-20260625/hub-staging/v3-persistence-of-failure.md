# Persistence of Failure
**Category:** The hub as a pressure release / pacing valve

## The idea
The hub is a room you walk through between dives. When you **succeed**, it visibly thrives (the inverse-sibling "visible growth" idea: lights on, vendors stocked, the yard fuller). This sibling is the **decay direction of that same hub-state system**: when you **miss quota** (or die, or time out), the hub *shows it*. A vendor's stall is shuttered and dark. A floodlight that was on last run is dead. A debt notice is nailed to the door. The bar you missed isn't an abstract `run_number → 1` and a number going red on a sell screen — it has a **visible home**, a place in the world that got worse because you came home short.

The pitch is **legibility of stakes**: a player who walks into a dimmer, emptier hub *feels* the failure as a state of their world, not as a counter they have to remember. Recovery is equally legible — re-light the lamp, the vendor comes back — so the room is also the most natural surface for **hope** after a stumble.

## What exists today
Honest read. Failure today does two very different things at two severities, and **neither has any visible home** — both are pure numbers:

- **Death / timeout (E3, `run_rules.gd`):** you keep a "pockets" subset (`pockets_fraction = 0.20`, highest-value-first) and lose the rest of the unbanked haul. Meta is untouched. Soft, per-run.
- **Quota miss (K2, `game_state.gd` `wipe_meta()`):** a full meta wipe — `money/salvage/lore/exposure/knowledge/recipes/banked_junk` reset, `run_number → 1`. Maximally harsh, Director-FINAL for K2 — but in direct tension with GDD §6 *"No total resets — the run resets, the life persists."*

There is **no hub scene at all** (a new scene per the brief), so failure currently surfaces only on the SellScreen and as state mutations. What's missing is a *place* that renders the consequence. Crucially, **what this room shows is downstream of the reset-severity dial (`p4`)** — p4 decides whether a miss is a wipe (`HARSH`), a counter-reset-plus-debt (`STANDARD`, recommended), or a grace-absorbed near-miss (`FORGIVING`). This room is the **visible expression of whatever p4 picks**: it doesn't decide severity, it *dramatizes* it.

## How it could fit in
A `Hub` scene reads its own meta-state on load and dresses the room from a small set of **decay markers**, the negative arm of the same hub-state system that "visible growth" drives positively:

- **Tie to the p4 dial.** In a **`STANDARD`/`FORGIVING`** build, decay is **recoverable** — a missed quota dims one lamp and posts a debt notice (mirroring `s5` debt-on-loss); clearing the next bar re-lights it. Decay is a *mood that lifts*, the visual half of "I'm behind, not dead." In a **`HARSH`** build, the decay markers become **the wipe made visible** — the vendors are gone because the wipe took them, the room resets to its bare Run-1 state, and the player *sees* what the counter zeroed.
- **Tie to grace/streak (`q4`).** A **warning state** (first miss under `grace_count`) is the perfect thing to render as a *threshold* visual rather than a consequence: a flickering lamp, a notice that says "clear next quota or you're wiped (1/1)" — the hub becomes q4's UI surface. A banked **streak token** could glow as a literal object on a shelf (your saved skip), spent and gone after it absorbs a miss.
- **Marker → state mapping (data-driven).** Each marker binds to a meta field threshold: `money < 0` → debt notice; `warnings_used > 0` → flickering lamp; consecutive-miss count → vendors leave one at a time; high `exposure` (GDD: *"High Exposure can lose you the yard"*) → a creditor/inspector presence. Authored as `.tres` decay rules so the Director tunes *how grim* without code.
- **Feature gating.** New `RunConfig` knob `hub_decay_enabled: bool = false`, off by default so the all-off control reproduces the current no-hub baseline; the default-play preset turns it on (mirrors `quota_enabled` gating). Stamp it on telemetry; new rows `hub_marker_shown` / `hub_marker_recovered` measure whether players *re-light the room* after a miss (the recovery-behavior metric p4 already wants).

## Research (cited)
**Darkest Dungeon's Hamlet** is the canonical "failure-as-place": stress doesn't fully heal between missions (capped at 100 on return), so the *town* carries the cost of bad runs and forces visible upkeep — failure persists in the home you return to, not just on a hero sheet ([Fandom](https://darkestdungeon.fandom.com/wiki/Stress), [Gemsbok](https://thegemsbok.com/art-reviews-and-articles/darkest-dungeon-red-hook-critique-mechanics-design/)). **Frostpunk's Discontent** escalates *visibly and spatially* — ignored problems become protests, then riots with new objectives, so the failure literally appears as people and events in your city, not a hidden meter ([Frostpunk Wiki](https://frostpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Discontent), [Book of Laws](https://frostpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Book_of_Laws)). **Animal Crossing's weeds** are the gentlest model — neglect grows visibly in your town, *"a grand public exhibition for your neglect,"* and clearing it is a small, warm recovery loop — proof that recoverable visible decay can read as motivating rather than punishing ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/let-me-keep-my-damn-weeds-animal-crossing-1843885508), [Nookipedia](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Weed)). The shared lesson: **a consequence rendered as a place you stand in is felt more than a counter** — and recoverable decay (AC) vs. terminal/escalating decay (Frostpunk) maps exactly onto our p4 forgiving↔harsh axis.

## Open questions
1. **Recoverable vs. permanent decay — Director/tone call.** Should every marker be re-lightable (AC model, recommended for `STANDARD`), or do some scars stay (a vendor who *left for good*)? Permanent markers add weight but risk a death-spiral feel. Recommend: recoverable by default, permanence only as a `HARSH`-preset flavor.
2. **How grim before it's demotivating? — fun/tone call, needs Director review.** A hub that gets *too* dark after one miss punishes the player twice (mechanically + emotionally) and can drive quit-after-loss — the exact churn p4 warns about. The GDD's "thriving vs. desperate surface" supports a *range*, but where the floor sits is a judgment call. Recommend a **shallow, legible** decay (1–2 markers per miss, fully recoverable) for Act 1, tested at the gate against session-continuation-after-loss.
3. **Hard dependency on p4.** This room cannot be specced until the reset-severity dial is dispositioned — its entire content (recoverable mood vs. visible wipe) flips on that one knob. Flag: **build p4's decision first**, then this room renders it.
4. **Does decay touch run-state or only meta?** Markers must read meta only (failure persists *because* meta persists); a death/timeout that costs only pockets should probably **not** dim the hub, or the soft loss reads as harshly as a quota wipe. Recommend markers fire on quota outcomes + debt/exposure, not on E3 within-run loss.
