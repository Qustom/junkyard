# A Departure Point
**Category:** The hub as run-selection / commitment

## The idea
The hub is a *place*, and somewhere in it sits a single physical exit into the bands — a rusted gate, a chained-up service door, a cobbled-together teleporter, or a tow-truck idling at the yard's edge. You can stand in the hub indefinitely: re-read intel, swap loadout, pay down debt, talk to Cyrus, stare at the gate. Nothing ticks. But the moment you walk *into* it, you are committed — the surface dissolves and the dive begins. There is no "Press Continue." The act of approaching and stepping through **is** the decision. Prep is free and untimed; departure is irrevocable. This puts the commitment beat in the player's feet, not in a menu, and pairs thematically with the one-way *extraction* commitment on the other end (`economy-extraction/e3-one-way-commitment.md`): you choose to go in by walking in, and choose to come out by reaching the exit alive.

## What exists today
A run starts purely in code: `GameState.start_run(band_id, seed)` reseeds `RNG`, builds a fresh `RunInventory`, and emits `EventBus.run_started`. The R0 staged-config seam (`stage_run_config()`, lines ~157-171) lets a caller pre-load a `RunConfig` (band, modifiers, feature flags) that the *next* `start_run` adopts, with an all-off default reproducing the M1.0 baseline. Today that call is triggered from the menu / `MainGame` boot — a button, effectively. The GDD already has the fiction for the diegetic version: a portal/surface-gate the player opens to drop into the yard's deeper bands. What's missing is the **hub scene itself** — there is no walkable surface space, so there is nowhere yet to put a door. The departure point is a NEW scene whose only mechanical job is to be the diegetic trigger for the existing `stage_run_config()` → `start_run()` pair.

## How it could fit in
A greybox `Area2D`/interactable in a new hub scene: walk onto it, get an interact prompt ("Step through?"), confirm, and it calls `GameState.stage_run_config(staged)` then `GameState.start_run(band, seed)` — the *same* seam the menu uses, so no engine rework, only a diegetic front-end. The staged config is assembled by the prep rooms the player visited first (loadout bench, intel board picks the band, a seed source). The gate sits at the hub's natural edge as the obvious "out." Feature-gate it behind a `RunConfig` flag so the all-off control still boots straight into a run (preserving the baseline), and the diegetic hub is opt-in until proven fun.

## Research (cited)
Walk-to-start departure points are a genre staple precisely because they make commitment felt. In **Hades**, Zagreus physically walks out the door of the House of Hades to begin each escape attempt — the threshold is the run-start, and the hub is unlimited downtime ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/hades-beginner-guide-tips), [Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2025/11/05/holism-why-hades-mattered/)). **Deep Rock Galactic** launches the squad from the Space Rig in a **Drop Pod** that drills down to Hoxxes — players gather at the pod and the descent is the irreversible go-signal ([DRG Wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Drop_Pod)). **Darkest Dungeon**'s stagecoach embark and **Monster Hunter**'s quest-board depart are the same beat: prep at the hub, then a single confirm sends you off — though MH notably lets you *abandon* a quest to undo it, forfeiting gains ([MH Rise manual](https://game.capcom.com/manual/MHRISE/en/switch/page/6/1)). **Returnal** ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/returnal-overgrown-ruins-walkthrough/)) shows the inverse: no hub door at all, just a hard cut into a re-rolled run — the contrast a departure point deliberately avoids. The throughline: the diegetic threshold converts an abstract "start" into an embodied, intentional commit.

## Open questions
- **Confirm prompt vs. instant?** A "Step through?" yes/no guards against fat-fingered commits but slightly re-introduces the menu beat; an instant step is purer but punishing. Recommend a confirm by default (this is a vision/fun call — **flag for Director**).
- **Can you change your mind?** Pure-commit (Hades) vs. MH-style abandon. The one-way framing argues pure-commit, but a short "still in the doorway, back out free" grace window may feel fairer. Overlaps `e3-one-way-commitment.md`.
- **One portal or many?** A single gate (config chosen in prep rooms) vs. multiple band-choice doors you walk to — the latter folds run-selection *into* the geometry and **overlaps c2/c3**; resolve which doc owns band-choice.
- **Scope:** building any walkable hub scene is net-new vs. the current menu-boot; needs a Director scope call on when (M2+?) a hub is worth the cost.
